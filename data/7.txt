**一、项目背景**
长视频平台会员市场激烈竞争，优酷致力于打造会员节日IP，通过线下月度主题活动+线上创意事件话题传播，联动微博、抖音、小红书KOL输出爆款内容，强化会员“权益+福利+体验”三大核心价值，夯实“超值”品牌心智，促进用户拉新与转化。

**二、核心策略**
9月：联动站内爆款IP《异人之下》，携手江西龙虎山鹰潭文旅，打造优酷SVIP会员“龙虎特邀体验团”。定制化游览路线、国风旅拍、名场面打卡等特色玩法，丰富会员追剧体验。现场预埋抖音小网红与天师互动事件，借势IP进行话题传播，提升品牌曝光。

11月：配合优酷片单发布及《甄嬛传》播出12周年，线上开启《甄嬛传》限时免费看，线下联动甄嬛传特展，打造优酷会员“甄学家”观展团。明星主创见面会、限定周边伴手礼、甄学家数字徽章等独家礼遇，强化会员身份标识。

12月：联动《TimeOut 北京》和5大雪场，推出“酷爱生活企划”。5大雪场门票免费领，还可享温暖礼包、热「雪」大片、限定特饮，打造冬日专属会员福利。

**三、项目成果**
微博话题 #优酷SVIP会员尊享日# 阅读量5000w+，互动量4w+。

话题 #优酷11月片单# 登微博热搜主榜第7位。

**1. Project Background**
In a fierce competition within the long-video membership system market, Youku focused on establishing a signature Membership Festival IP. By integrating monthly offline themed activities with creative online viral campaigns, we collaborated with KOLs across Weibo, Douyin, and Xiaohongshu to produce high-impact content. The strategy aimed to reinforce three core values—Benefits, Perks, and Experience—solidifying the brand's "High Cost-effectiveness Value" identity and driving user acquisition and conversion.
 
**2. Core Strategy**
September Strategy: Collaborating with the hit IP "I Am Nobody", we partnered with Jiangxi Longhu Mountain Tourism to launch the "SVIP Exclusive Experience Tour." We enriched the membership experience through customized travel routes, Chinese Style photography, and iconic scene recreations. By orchestrating interaction between Douyin influencers and local Taoist characters, we leveraged IP influence to amplify brand exposure.

November Strategy: To coincide with the 12th anniversary of "Empresses in the Palace", we offered limited-time free streaming online and collaborated with an offline special exhibition for the "Zhen-Experts" Fan Tour. Exclusive privileges, such as meet-and-greets with the cast, limited-edition merchandise, and digital badges, were designed to strengthen membership identity.

December Strategy: Partnering with TimeOut Beijing and five major ski resorts, we launched the "For the Love of Life." Members received free admission and exclusive "Winter Warmth" packages, professional ski photography, and limited-edition drinks, creating a unique seasonal benefit ecosystem.

**3. Key Achievements**
The Weibo hashtag #Youku SVIP Member Day# generated 50 million+ views and 40k+ interactions.

The topic #Youku November Watchlist# peaked at No.7 on the Weibo Hot Search Main List.
