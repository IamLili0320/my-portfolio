**一、项目背景**
说唱歌手弹壳参加芒果TV综艺《披荆斩棘》第三季。比赛期间，借助节目曝光，通过精准挖掘艺人舞台亮点与性格魅力，打造高热话题，助力艺人破圈扩声。

**二、核心策略**
传播形象定位：基于艺人粉丝画像与节目受众分层，反向锚定“三位一体”艺人形象。核心圈层强化专业硬实力，次核心圈层塑造社交亲和力，大众圈层输出反差萌与生活化特质。同一人设，分层透传。

传播内容设计：针对不同圈层设计差异化传播看点。说唱粉需要“崇拜感”，以高光舞台和业务能力固粉；节目粉需要“关系共情”，以成员间温情互动实现粉丝转化；大众需要“亲近感”，以幽默和生活化片段拉近距离。由内向外，逐级破圈。

创意物料企划：每期从舞台表演、后台花絮、社交互动中抓取高光帧与情绪点。官号打造艺宣类创意海报；KOL矩阵输出表情包、叠字图、动图等轻物料助推传播。形成“素材提取 - 话题预埋 - 物料分层发放 - 热搜破圈”标准化传播闭环。

**三、项目成果**
项目周期内，艺人相关话题累计登上全网热搜42+次，全网话题讨论量42w+。

**1. Project Background**
Rapper Danko participated in Mango TV's "Call Me by Fire S3". Leveraging the show's high-profile exposure, we identified the artist's stage highlights and personality charms to create trending topics, effectively expanding his reach beyond the core rap community.
 
**2. Core Strategy**
Image Positioning: Based on fan profiling and audience segmentation, we anchored a "triad-style" artist persona. We emphasized professional competence for the core fanbase, social charisma for the casual viewers, and contrasting charm for the general public—delivering a unified yet multi-layered identity.

Content Design: Customized narratives were designed for different circles: providing "Sense of Awe" for rap fans through high-performance stages; building "Relational Empathy" for program fans via heartfelt member interactions; and offering "Approachability" for the public through humorous, slice-of-life snippets. This allowed for a progressive cross-community expansion.

Creative Asset Planning: Extracted high-impact frames and emotional cues from each episode's performance and backstage footage. Official channels focused on high-end posters, while a KOL matrix circulated "lightweight assets" like memes, kinetic typography, and GIFs to drive virality, forming a standardized loop from topic seeding to trending outcome.

**3. Key Achievements**
During the project cycle, artist-related topics reached the Top Trends (Hot Search) over 42+ times across all platforms, generating more than 420K+ social discussions nationwide.
