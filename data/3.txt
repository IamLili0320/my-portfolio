**一、项目背景**
结合SFK夏季国际艺术教育展，打造线上学生毕业作品展，提升存量用户黏性，触达潜在留学客群，夯实品牌专业认知，放大品牌声量。

**二、核心策略**
活动机制设计：采用“作品展示+每日限额投票+先锋创作人评选”的组合机制，鼓励学生参与报名，激励亲友持续拉票，形成社交裂变，同时控制刷票风险。

传播机制构建：公众号发布活动预告与评选结果，建立信息触达广度；小程序活动页展示学员作品，生成专属投票海报，降低分享门槛。借助私域传播，触达潜在留学群体，实现“信任传递 → 留资 → 转化”的闭环。

创意主题企划：2024年主题“我向艺术的告白”，聚焦青年创作者个体情感与自我表达；2025年主题“经纬之间”，回应艺术教育跨学科发展趋势。主题贯穿主视觉、投票海报及衍生物料，提升品牌识别与情感共鸣。

**三、项目成果**
活动参与人数200+，投票人数4w+。

**1. Project Background**
In conjunction with the SFK Summer International Art Education Exhibition, we launched an online Graduation Exhibition. The initiative aimed to deepen engagement within our existing community, reach potential clients, and reinforce the brand's professional authority while amplifying market presence.

**2. Core Strategy**
Mechanism Design: A tiered structure combining "Artwork Showcase+Daily Limited Voting+Creator Awards." This system encouraged student enrollment and incentivized consistent social sharing, driving organic social fission while maintaining fair-play controls.

Communication Architecture: We utilized WeChat Official Accounts for broad awareness and Mini-Programs for interactive engagement. By generating personalized voting posters, we lowered the barrier for social sharing. Leveraging private traffic, we established a conversion loop: "Trust Building → Lead Generation → Final Conversion."

Creative Theme Planning: The 2024 theme, "My Endearment to Art," focused on individual emotional expression; the 2025 theme, "Between Longitude and Latitude," reflected the interdisciplinary evolution of art education. The themes unified the visual identity and promotional collateral, strengthening brand recognition and emotional resonance.

**3. Key Achievements**
Successfully attracted 200+ participating artists and generated over 40K+ unique votes.
