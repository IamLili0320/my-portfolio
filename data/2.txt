**一、项目背景**
《向往的生活》“田野音乐会”特辑播出后反响热烈。作为吕思清老师的经纪方，我们与综艺团队萌生了开发一档原创音乐综艺的想法，并从音乐角度提供了完整的创意思路。

**二、核心创意** 
我们惯于用视觉和味觉去描述一座城，却忽略了每座城都有自己的呼吸——那些我们习以为常，却后知后觉，在另一座城市不会再听到的声音。

声音采集： 捕捉地方传统音乐、街边叫卖、市井烟火等城市特色声音碎片，串联集体记忆，重构地域文化的听觉叙事。

跨界共生： 邀请古典音乐家、流行音乐人与城市飞行嘉宾，共同寻找声音碎片，创作城市主题曲，打造城市新文化IP。

街头快闪： 成员突袭街头上演即兴音乐会，致敬城市中每一位努力生活的普通人，“今天你也辛苦了”。

**三、项目成果**
创意获得《向往的生活》团队与爱奇艺平台的高度认可，并成功入选2022年爱奇艺片单，但受制于疫情与招商环境，项目遗憾未能制作上线。

**1. Project Background**
Following the success of the "Field Concert" special on "Back to Field S5", we collaborated with the production team to conceptualize an original music show. As the management of Siqing Lu, we crafted a creative framework from a professional musical perspective.

**2. Creative Concept**
We are accustomed to defining a city through sight and taste, often overlooking its "breath"—the familiar sounds that we only truly notice once they are gone.

Field Recording: Capturing the sound fragments of a city—traditional melodies, street cries, and the bustle of daily life, to reconnect collective memories and reconstruct a regional auditory narrative.

Cross-sector Synergy: Inviting classical virtuosos, pop musicians, and "city flyers" to seek for sound fragments, co-creating city anthems that establish a new urban cultural IP.

Pop-up Concerts: Performances dedicated to the everyday heroes of the city, offering a musical embrace, "You've worked hard today."

**3. Key Achievements**
The concept received high acclaim from the Back to Field team and iQIYI, successfully securing a spot on iQIYI's 2022 Watchlist. However, due to pandemic-related challenges and the shifting investment landscape, the project was ultimately shelved before production.
