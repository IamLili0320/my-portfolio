**一、项目背景**
SFK国际艺术教育展于每年春、夏、秋三季于全国12座城市巡回开启，通过“讲座+咨询+学生作品展”的形式，叠加多重签约福利，加速客户决策，推动现场签约转化。

**二、核心策略**
差异化活动运营：基于各分校客户资源与签约数据，定制差异化活动方案与阶梯式预算，精准控制ROI，提升签约转化率。

痛点式内容设计：围绕市场趋势、客户留咨痛点及SFK学员录取案例，拆解留学需求，制定专题讲座，实现精准答疑。以就业为抓手，邀请百度、阿里、完美世界等行业精英及SFK大厂校友，构建“艺术留学-实习-就业”闭环对话场景。

全渠道传播矩阵：公众号+官网深度拆解展会亮点与到场价值，建立专业信任；小程序发布活动邀约，开放大咖及院校代表咨询报名，沉淀高意向线索；小红书+抖音+视频号以年轻化内容精准触达目标圈层，放大活动声量。

**三、项目成果**
全国活动参与人数4000+，签约总金额6000w+。

**1. Project Background**
The SFK International Art Education Exhibition is held across 12 cities nationwide during the spring, summer, and autumn seasons. By integrating "seminars+consultations+student artwork display" with multi-tiered signing incentives, the project accelerates client decision-making and drives on-site conversion.
 
**2. Core Strategy**
Activity Operations: Based on regional branch client resources and historical contract data, we customized differentiated activity plans and stepped budgets to precisely control ROI and maximize signing rates.

Pain-point Driven Content: We deconstructed study-abroad demands through market trends and admission case studies. Using career prospects as a key lever, we invited industry elites from Baidu, Alibaba, and Perfect World, along with SFK alumni from leading tech firms, to establish a "Study-Internship-Career" closed-loop dialogue.

Omni-channel Communication: WeChat and the official website were used to provide in-depth analysis of exhibition highlights; the Mini-program managed registrations and captured high-intent leads; while Xiaohongshu, Douyin, and Video Accounts engaged target demographics with youth-oriented content to amplify brand presence.

**3. Key Achievements**
Total participants exceeded 4,000+ nationwide, generating a total contract value of over 60 million RMB.
