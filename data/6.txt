**一、项目背景**
优酷深耕人文赛道11年，是全网“第一”和“唯一”深耕文化频道的平台。11周年之际，打造文化领域出圈事件，深化“看文化人物 上优酷”的品牌心智。

**二、核心策略**
线下：绑定北京网络视听艺术大会，联动站内人文IP资源，打造“优酷人文雅集”。围绕“看见风雅、看见历史、看见女性、看见文学”四大主题板块，邀请单霁翔、陈铭、刘仪伟、鸟鸟等14位文化人物齐聚通州水畔，共话人文新篇。

线上：预热期，以人文内核输出创意物料。主题人物海报打造优酷人文排面，回顾短片《需要人文的理由》以 15+ IP节目金句串联11年人文记忆；爆发期，预埋话题切中大众情绪。嘉宾金句海报定格高光发言时刻；主题访谈视频回应网络热议，输出人文新见解。

**三、项目成果**
20家党央媒体、8家行业媒体深度报道，13家大V齐发声。微博相关传播话题累计阅读量 2000w+，互动量2w+。

话题 #鸟鸟说亲密关系就像自己和猫的相处# 登微博热搜主榜上升趋势，相关提问登知乎热榜TOP18。

**1. Project Background**
As the No.1 and only platform dedicated to cultural channel for 11 years, Youku celebrated its anniversary by launching a nationwide cultural sensation. The project aimed to reinforce the brand's core identity: "To see cultural icons, choose Youku."
 
**2. Core Strategy**
Offline Strategy: In collaboration with the Beijing Internet Audio-Visual Art Convention, we integrated premium IP resources to host the "Youku Humanities Gathering". Centered on four themes—"Elegance, History, Women, and Literature"—the event brought together 14 cultural icons by the riverside in Tongzhou Beijing to envision the future of humanities.

Online Strategy: During the warm-up phase, based on Youku's humanity inner core, we released creative character posters and a retrospective film, "The Reason Why We Need Humanities", which linked 11 years of memories using quotes from 15+ top-tier IPs. During the peak phase, we curated emotional topics and "Golden Quote" posters to capture highlight moments, while exclusive interviews addressed trending topics with fresh cultural insights.

**3. Key Achievements**
The campaign received in-depth coverage from 20 central state media outlets and 8 industry publications. Social media engagement exceeded 20 million views and 20k interactions. 

The trending topic #Niaoniao on Intimacy# climbed the Weibo Hot Search Main List, while related discussions reached Top 18 on Zhihu. 

The short film peaked at Top 3 on nationwide hot lists, and the Douyin campaign produced a viral hit with 33k+ likes.
