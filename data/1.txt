**一、项目背景**
作为北京CBD国际商务季的核心板块之一，北京CBD音乐季旨在以音乐为媒介，提升区域文化氛围，赋能商圈消费生态，打造CBD活力新磁极。

**二、核心创意**
拉近古典音乐与大众的距离，让音乐走进城市日常，让生活成为流动的舞台。

携手吕思清、秦立巍等国际顶级音乐家，将高品质音乐演出引入城市空间，制造不期而遇的音乐现场；合作钢琴品牌The One，在商圈落地音乐角，赋能商圈消费；联动媒体传播创意内容，提振CBD艺术文化声量；组建CBD合唱团，通过音乐服务与人才建设，形成可持续的社区文化生态。

**三、项目成果**
现象级传播：开幕音乐会联合人民日报、新华社，全网直播观看量200万+；

演出新场景：落地10场+城市音乐演出，涵盖CBD数字会客厅·元宇宙音乐会、今日美术馆·奇梦幻境音乐会、银泰中心新空间音乐会、CBD咖啡青年节·阿卡贝拉音乐路演等；

创意内容破圈：合作京港地铁14号线，发布创意短片《北京CBD音乐季律动14号线商业带》；中国日报、新京报、三联生活周刊等主流媒体深度报道，全网观看量7.6万+。

**1. Project Background**
As a core segment of the Beijing CBD International Business Season, the project leverages music as a medium to enhance the regional cultural atmosphere, empower the commercial consumption ecosystem, and create a new magnetic pole of vitality for the CBD.

**2. Creative Concept**
Dedicated to collapsing the distance between classical music and the audience by integrating it into the city's daily rhythm.

Partnered with world-renowned musicians such as Siqing Lu and Li-Wei Qin to bring high-fidelity performances into urban spaces, creating unexpected musical encounters; Collaborated with the piano brand The One to install "Music Corners" across the district, boosting consumption vitality; Synergized with media platforms to disseminate creative content, amplifying the CBD's voice online; Formed the CBD Choir to foster community culture through musical service and talent cultivation, ensuring long-term sustainability.

**3. Key Achievements**
Phenomenal Communication: The opening concert, in partnership with People's Daily and Xinhua News Agency, achieved 2M+ views through global live streaming.

Immersive Spatial Innovations: Executed 10+ curated live music performances in unconventional venues, including the CBD Meta-Universe Concert, Today's Museum "Dreamscape" Concert, Beijing Yintai Centre "The New Space Concert" and the A Cappella Roadshow at the CBD Coffee Youth Festival.

Cross-Sector Contents: Collaborated with MTR Line 14 to release the creative short film Rhythm of Line 14. The project received in-depth coverage from top-tier media such as China Daily, garnering 76k+ views across all platforms.
